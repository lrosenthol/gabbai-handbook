# Half Kaddish / חֲצִי קַדִּישׁ

Said by the *shaliach tzibbur* to mark a transition within the service: before *Barchu*, before the *Amidah* at *Mincha* and *Maariv*, and at *Shacharit* after *Tachanun* or *Hallel*, before the Torah reading.  The lines marked *Aseret Yemei Teshuvah* (in yellow) replace the line printed just above them during the Ten Days of Repentance (*Aseret Yemei Teshuvah*), from Rosh Hashanah through Yom Kippur.

**At the Torah reading**, the order depends on the day:

- **Shabbat and Yom Tov morning, one scroll:** after the last required aliyah, *hagbah* and *gelilah*, then half-Kaddish, then the maftir is called.
- **When the maftir is read from a second scroll:** half-Kaddish is said after the last required aliyah with both scrolls on the bimah, *before* *hagbah* and *gelilah* of the first scroll; then the maftir is called.
- **Days with no maftir (weekdays, Shabbat *Mincha*, fast days):** after the last aliyah, *hagbah* and *gelilah*, then half-Kaddish, before the scroll is returned to the ark.

Customs differ on the exact order, so follow the practice of your congregation.  See *Calling the Maftir* (the Kaddish step) and *When More Than One Torah Is Read* in the *Torah Reading* chapter.

יִתְבָּרַךְ וְיִשְׁתַּבַּח וְיִתְפָּאַר וְיִתְרוֹמַם וְיִתְנַשֵּׂא וְיִתְהַדָּר וְיִתְעַלֶּה וְיִתְהַלָּל שְׁמֵהּ דְקוּדְשָׁא בְּרִיךְ הוּא. (קהל: בְּרִיךְ הוּא)
